## 1 — Launch

Screen Privacy now has Alarm mode 🚨

Look away → countdown → armed.
Movement can trigger a siren + flashing ALARM screen.

Free, open source, local processing. macOS 14+.
A deterrent—not a lock or a theft guarantee.

## 2 — Coffee scenario

Working from a café? Here’s the idea:

You go to collect your coffee. Screen Privacy arms after a countdown. Someone moves your MacBook → it can sound a siren and flash ALARM.

Test your setup first. Keep valuables with you when possible.

## 3 — Short-break scenario

A quick toilet break shouldn’t mean leaving your work on display.

Screen Privacy covers your screen when you look away. Optional Alarm mode can sound a siren if movement—or camera loss—is detected after arming.

Lock your Mac too 🔒
